# 02 · Dictation

Tap the big microphone and talk. Words appear as you speak, on the phone, offline.

**Try saying:** “Pick up milk comma bread and apples full stop new paragraph Remember to call Sam full stop”

**Spoken commands** while dictating:
- “new paragraph”, “new line”, “new bullet”
- “full stop” (or “period”), “comma”, “question mark”, “exclamation mark”
- “scratch that” removes the last sentence

**Fix a word:** if a word is highlighted in yellow, tap it to hear that moment and choose the right word. For example, if “pear” was heard as “pair”, tap the highlighted word and correct it. “Um” and “uh” are left out (Settings › Dictation).

Long-press the microphone to choose whether to keep audio, or to use **Immersive Captions**.

A dictated note is initially titled with its first words and has no heading. Accept a suggested title (or type one), and a `# Title` heading is added above your words without changing the dictated text.

**Try it:** while inside this linked folder, dictate a short note. After saving, refresh if needed and check for the new Markdown file in GitHub's `guide/` folder.
